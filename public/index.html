Make the conversation more visual.

Dental aesthetic treatment can be difficult to imagine from words alone. SIMOURA is designed to turn a patient's existing smile photograph into a visual starting point for discussing potential aesthetic directions.

When the conversation needs more precision.

SIMOURA can use a separate AI-assisted workflow to identify individually analyzable teeth using standard FDI numbering. A dentist can select a Kimi-approved tooth, review its independent analysis, and create a focused visualization.
